This site distributes city-centre records derived from GeoNames via
geonamescache 3.0.2 in data/places.json. It includes 34,006 city records.

Changes made for this site: selection and validation of records, JSON column
reformatting, normalized search aliases, additional familiar Chinese city and
country display names, composed city-centre labels, and duplicate-name labels
with coordinates. The underlying city-centre coordinates and time-zone IDs
are taken from the source records. GeoNames does not endorse this site.

The separate geonamescache Python package is licensed under MIT. Its original
copyright and permission notice are retained in geonamescache-MIT.txt. The MIT
software license does not replace the CC BY 4.0 attribution for GeoNames data.
